Home row mods

Hover any key with a purple hold legend on Base or Extra. The board dims every position that cannot complete that hold — the rest is literally the behavior's hold-trigger-key-positions list.

Left-hand mods only engage when the next key is on the right hand or a thumb, and vice versa. That positional rule, not the idle timeout, is what stops a mod firing mid-roll.

Switching base layer

The Base / Extra / Tap keys on the top row of the held layers keep Miryoku's double-tap guard — a tap-dance whose first binding is &none. They need two taps, marked 2×.

Tap is the way out of hold-taps entirely: no mods, no layers. Its outer right thumb is the only exit.
